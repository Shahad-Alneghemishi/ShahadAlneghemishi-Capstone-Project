# Verification Record

| Scenario | Expected result | Actual result | Status |
|----------|-----------------|---------------|--------|
| Main workflow: a new customer registers, logs in, adds a device, requests a service | The order is saved with status Requested and appears in their order list | A customer can register, log in, add a device and request two services. The order appeared in the list with status Requested, its services and the correct total. Inactive services are not offered, and a customer with no devices is sent to the add-device page. Staff can assign an active technician and change the status; marking an order Delivered fills the completion date automatically, and once an order is In Progress the customer no longer sees the Cancel link | Pass |
| Missing required fields and invalid values: registration with an existing phone, an existing username or a weak password; device form with an empty brand; order form without a service; service with a negative price; staff moving an order past Requested without a technician | Clear error messages, nothing is saved | Registration cases and the empty brand showed the expected errors and nothing was saved. Order form without a service: the first run showed a success message although nothing was saved (the message and redirect were outside the validity check). After the fix it shows "This field is required", shows no success message and stays on the form. A service with a negative price in the admin was rejected with the message "Constraint servicetype_price_non_negative is violated". Moving an order to In Progress without a technician was rejected with the message "Assign a technician before moving the order past Requested" and nothing was saved; the technician list does not offer the inactive technician | Pass |
| Search and filters: as staff, search orders by customer or device and filter by status, once with matches and once with none | Matching orders are listed; an empty-state message appears when there are none | The staff order list shows all customers' orders. Searching "noura" lists only her orders, searching "zzz" shows "No orders match your search.", and filtering by Delivered lists only the delivered orders. The Next and Previous links move between pages and keep the search and status filter | Pass |
| Updates and deletion with dependencies: delete a device that has orders; cancel an order | Deleting a device with orders is refused with a clear message; only Requested orders can be cancelled | Deleting a device with orders was refused with the message "This device has service orders, so it cannot be deleted" and the device stayed in the list. A device without orders was deleted, and editing a device saved the change. A Requested order was cancelled after a confirmation page. Cancelled orders show no cancel link, and opening the cancel URL of an already cancelled order showed "This order can no longer be cancelled" and kept its status. Staff cannot edit a cancelled order: it has no Update link and typing its URL shows a message. The technician list offers only active technicians | Pass |
